## Why ChirpTime exists

1. What do you actually go back and watch? (a) "what did I do / how long did X take", (b) grab a clip of something that happened (a bug, a moment, a call), (c) devlog / film raw footage, (d) accountability / focus, (e) it just runs and you rarely look. Rank them. The viewer gets designed around #1.
2. When you open a session, how do you find the moment? Scrub by eye, remember the time, or search for something said/typed? (Recommendation: design for "scan a visual strip of the day, then zoom in.")
3. Is the 4-screen + webcam + mic + system-audio setup the only rig, or should the design hold for a laptop with one screen and no webcam too?
4. Who else ever sees this footage? Only you, or do exports go to people (team, YouTube, the devlog films)?

## Recording model

5. The interval "chirp" mode (screenshot + 5 s clip every N minutes) last ran 2026-08-02; continuous sessions are all you use. Kill the interval mode and its calendar/gallery entirely? (Rec: yes. One mode: sessions. Keep the chirp sound as the "recording started/stopped" cue.)
6. Markers: 0 used across 30 sessions. Drop them, or make them worth using (a global hotkey that drops a named bookmark)? (Rec: global hotkey, e.g. Ctrl+Alt+M, plus auto-markers from app switches.)
7. Should recording start on its own: at login, or when you're active and stop when idle? (Rec: a "start at login" option, plus the idle trimming that already exists.)
8. Tray icon instead of an always-on-top widget? (Rec: tray-first, with the widget as an optional floating pill you can hide. The 320×540 always-on-top panel is a lot of screen to give up for something that runs all day.)
9. Many sessions are 0–3 minutes long (false starts). Auto-discard sessions under, say, 60 s? (Rec: yes, with an undo toast.)

## Delete a block of time (priority one)

10. Destructive or reversible? (Rec: the cut takes effect instantly and is hidden from playback; whole 60 s segments inside the range are deleted from disk immediately to free space; the two edge segments are trimmed at keyframes in the background. One "Undo" for ~10 s, then it's permanent. No trash bin of 20 GB.)
11. Does a delete apply to every track (screens, webcam, mic, system audio) at once? (Rec: yes by default, with an advanced "only these tracks" option, e.g. drop the mic for a private call but keep the screens.)
12. How should selecting 3 hours out of 8 feel? (Rec: drag on a zoomed-out day strip with live thumbnails; snap to idle gaps and activity changes; I / O keys set in and out at the playhead; then Delete.)
13. Do you want "delete everything before/after the playhead" and "delete all idle stretches longer than N min" as one-click actions too? (Rec: yes, both.)
14. Can a delete split one session into two, or does the session keep a hole? (Rec: keep a hole, shown as a collapsed cut on the timeline.)

## Export

15. Output: one composed MP4 with the layout you pick, or also separate per-track files? (Rec: composed MP4 is the main path; "raw tracks" as a secondary option.)
16. Layout presets you'd want: single screen; screen + webcam bubble; side-by-side screens; full 4-screen wall; webcam only. Anything else? Vertical 9:16 for Shorts?
17. Target sizes: 1080p / 1440p / 4K, plus "small enough to paste in Slack / Discord" (e.g. under 25 MB)? (Rec: three presets: Original, 1080p, Share (≤25 MB).)
18. Speed-up in export (timelapse a 2-hour stretch into 2 minutes)? (Rec: yes, a speed control per export, and idle stretches auto-skipped.)
19. Audio in export: mic, system, both, with a volume per track? Burn in captions from a transcript?
20. Where do exports go, and should "copy to clipboard as file" be the default action when it finishes?

## Viewer / library

21. The library: a list, or a visual calendar of days with a thumbnail strip per session? (Rec: a day timeline, one row per day, sessions as bars with thumbnails, total hours per week.)
22. Storage: 30 sessions are already 174 GB, and the webcam is the biggest track (24 GB of an 8.5 h session, more than all 4 screens together). Want a storage view with "free space by: dropping webcam after N days, downscaling old sessions, deleting sessions older than N"? (Rec: yes, with retention rules off by default. Separately I'll look at why the webcam costs so much.)
23. The 4 screens: do you watch them as a wall, or almost always one at a time? (Rec: default to "follow focus", the screen where the mouse was, with the wall one key away.)
24. Should the app learn which apps/windows were active (window titles over time) so the timeline can show "VS Code 2h 10m, Chrome 40m"? It's cheap to record and makes the timeline searchable.
25. Transcription: the recorder already writes a 16 kHz mic WAV "for later transcription" and nothing uses it. Build local transcription (Whisper) so you can search what you said? (Rec: yes, phase 2, local only.)
